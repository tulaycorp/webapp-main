**AUTOMATED VERIFICATION:**

All orders undergo automated fraud detection screening:
- Address verification system (AVS) check
- Card verification value (CVV) validation
- Billing/shipping address cross-reference
- Purchase pattern analysis
- Geo-location verification
- Device fingerprinting
- Velocity checks (multiple orders from same source)

**MANUAL REVIEW REQUIREMENTS:**

Orders may be flagged for manual review if:
- First-time customer with high-value order ($500+)
- Billing and shipping addresses in different countries
- Multiple failed payment attempts
- Unusual purchase patterns
- Shipping to freight forwarder or high-risk location
- Mismatched cardholder name and recipient name
- Expedited shipping to international destination

**VERIFICATION PROCEDURES:**

Flagged orders may require:
- Additional identity verification documents
- Photo ID matching credit card name
- Bank statement confirmation
- Phone verification call
- Email confirmation of order details
- Cardholder authorization form

**VERIFICATION TIMEFRAMES:**

- Verification requests sent via email within 2 hours of order placement
- Customer response required within 24 hours
- Manual review completed within 1-2 business days
- Orders not verified within 48 hours are automatically cancelled

**ORDER CANCELLATION FOR FRAUD:**

FRAMEWORK SUPPLY CO. reserves the right to cancel orders suspected of fraudulent  
activity without notification. Cancelled orders receive full refunds within 5-7  
business days.

## 6. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING, CUSTOMS & DUTIES

### 6.1 INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING OVERVIEW

FRAMEWORK SUPPLY CO. offers international shipping to over 60 countries worldwide.  
International orders are subject to additional regulations, fees, and delivery  
timeframes compared to domestic shipments.

**INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING RESPONSIBILITIES:**

- **FRAMEWORK Responsibilities:**
  - Accurate customs documentation
  - Proper product declaration and valuation
  - Carrier selection and shipment booking
  - Export compliance
  - Tracking provision
  
- **Customer Responsibilities:**
  - Payment of customs duties and taxes
  - Import compliance with destination country
  - Customs clearance facilitation
  - Accurate address provision
  - Timely package pickup/acceptance

### 6.2 CUSTOMS DOCUMENTATION & DECLARATIONS

**REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION:**

All international shipments include:
- Commercial invoice (3 copies minimum)
- Customs declaration form (CN22 or CN23)
- Harmonized System (HS) tariff codes
- Country of origin declaration
- Product description and composition
- Item value and currency
- Shipping terms (DAP - Delivered at Place)

**PRODUCT VALUATION:**

- Products declared at actual sale price
- Currency conversion at time of shipment
- Discounts and promotions reflected in declared value
- Shipping costs excluded from merchandise value
- Gift declarations limited by destination country regulations

**RESTRICTED GOODS DECLARATION:**

Certain products may require additional documentation:
- Textile and apparel certificates
- Brand authentication documentation
- Fiber content declarations
- Care labeling compliance certificates

### 6.3 CUSTOMS DUTIES, TAXES & FEES

**CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY:**

**IMPORTANT:** Customers are solely responsible for all customs duties, taxes,  
and fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are collected by  
the carrier or customs authority upon delivery or clearance.

**TYPES OF CHARGES:**

- **Customs Duties:** Import tariffs based on product type and value
- **Value Added Tax (VAT):** Applicable in EU, UK, and other jurisdictions
- **Goods and Services Tax (GST):** Applicable in Canada, Australia, etc.
- **Brokerage Fees:** Carrier fees for customs clearance services
- **Handling Fees:** Additional carrier or customs processing fees

**ESTIMATING DUTIES & TAXES:**

Duty and tax rates vary by:
- Destination country
- Product category (HS code)
- Product value
- Country of origin
- Trade agreements

Estimated duties/taxes calculators:
- **EU:** https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/
- **Canada:** https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/
- **Australia:** https://www.abf.gov.au/
- **UK:** https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad

**DUTY-FREE THRESHOLDS:**

Many countries have duty-free import thresholds (de minimis values):
- Canada: CAD $20
- European Union: €150 (as of July 2021)
- United Kingdom: £135
- Australia: AUD $1,000
- United Kingdom: USD $800

Orders below these thresholds may be exempt from duties (taxes may still apply).

**REFUSED DUTIES & TAXES:**

If a customer refuses to pay duties/taxes:
- Package returned to FRAMEWORK SUPPLY CO.
- Customer charged return shipping costs
- Refund issued minus shipping costs and restocking fee (20%)
- Original shipping charges non-refundable
