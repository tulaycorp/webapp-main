## 1. INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW

Welcome to **FRAMEWORK SUPPLY CO.** ("Company," "we," "us," "our," or "FRAMEWORK").

experience on our website and mobile applications. This comprehensive Privacy

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE DIRECTLY

- **Account Registration Information:** Name, email address, password, date of birth,  
  phone number, and security questions when you create an account
- **Shipping & Billing Information:** Physical addresses, phone numbers, and delivery  
  preferences for order fulfillment and our Free Shipping program (orders over $150)
- **Payment Information:** Credit card numbers, debit card details, digital wallet  
  information, billing addresses, and payment transaction history. Payment  
  processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant third-party processors
- **Customer Communications:** Messages, inquiries, feedback, product reviews,  
  testimonials, and responses to surveys or questionnaires
- **Customer Support Data:** Chat transcripts, email support correspondence, support  
  ticket information, and call recordings with your consent for our 24/7 Support  
  services
- **Social Media Profiles:** If you link social media accounts or authenticate through  
  social platforms
- **Preference & Interest Data:** Style preferences, size information, notification  
  preferences, wish lists, and product interest indicators
- **User-Generated Content:** Photos, videos, comments, and social posts where you  
  mention or feature FRAMEWORK products

### 2.2 INFORMATION COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

- **Device Information:** IP address, device type, operating system, browser type,  
  device identifiers, mobile network information, and unique device IDs
- **Browsing Behavior:** Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, search  
  queries, product views, cart contents (even if not purchased), and navigation  
  patterns
- **Cookies & Similar Technologies:** Session IDs, persistent cookies, web beacons,  
  pixels, and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience
- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates (with permission), IP-based location inference,  
  and geolocation data used for local delivery optimization and targeted marketing
- **Biometric Data:** Fingerprint or facial recognition data if you use biometric  
  authentication on our mobile app
- **Error & Performance Data:** Crash reports, system logs, application performance  
  metrics, and diagnostic information
- **Referral Data:** Information about how you were referred to our platform  
  (affiliate links, promotional codes, marketing campaigns)

### 2.3 INFORMATION FROM THIRD PARTIES

- **Social Media Platforms:** Profile information when you authenticate through social networks
- **Payment Processors:** Transaction verification, fraud detection results, and payment status
- **Shipping & Logistics Partners:** Delivery status, tracking information, and carrier data
- **Marketing & Analytics Partners:** Aggregate behavioral data, market research insights,  
  and performance metrics
- **Data Brokers & Aggregators:** Publicly available information for verification and risk  
  assessment purposes
- **Business Partners:** Co-marketing partners, affiliates, and strategic collaborators may  
  share customer information with your consent

## 4. DATA SECURITY & PROTECTION MEASURES

### 4.1 TECHNICAL SAFEGUARDS

- **Encryption:** All data in transit uses TLS/SSL encryption protocols. Sensitive data  
  at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption standards
- **Firewalls & Intrusion Detection:** Multi-layered firewall architecture with real-time  
  intrusion detection and prevention systems
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access control (RBAC) limiting employee access to  
  personally identifiable information
- **Database Security:** Secure database configurations, parameterized queries, and  
  protection against SQL injection attacks
- **API Security:** OAuth 2.0 authentication, API rate limiting, and request validation protocols
- **Vulnerability Management:** Regular security scanning, penetration testing, and patch management

### 4.2 ADMINISTRATIVE SAFEGUARDS

- Background checks and security training for all employees handling personal data
- Documented security policies and incident response procedures
- Regular security awareness training and phishing simulations
- Vendor security assessments and third-party compliance verification
- Data minimization principles to collect only necessary information

### 4.3 PHYSICAL SAFEGUARDS

- Secure data centers with 24/7 monitoring and access logging
- Environmental controls (temperature, humidity) to protect equipment
- Video surveillance and badge access systems
- Secure document destruction and device disposal procedures

### 4.4 LIMITATIONS OF SECURITY

While we implement comprehensive security measures, no method of transmission over  
the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee  
absolute security of your personal information. You are responsible for  
maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for any  
activities occurring under your account.

## 5. COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

### 5.1 TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

- **Essential Cookies:** Required for authentication, account security, and basic  
  website functionality. These cannot be disabled
- **Performance Cookies:** Measure website performance, user engagement, and error tracking
- **Functional Cookies:** Remember user preferences, language settings, accessibility  
  choices, and login information
- **Marketing Cookies:** Track conversions, enable retargeting, and personalize advertisements
- **Analytics Cookies:** Collect aggregated usage data for website optimization and business intelligence

### 5.2 SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

- Web Beacons & Pixels
- Local Storage & Session Storage
- Mobile Identifiers
- Cross-Device Tracking

### 5.3 OPTING OUT OF COOKIES

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies  
may impair website functionality. You may also opt out via:

- Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): www.optout.networkadvertising.org  
- Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): www.aboutads.info/choices  
- Browser “Do Not Track” signals (if supported)
